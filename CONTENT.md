# Content notes

The café and conversation-repair lessons added in v0.7 are original editorial compositions, not dictionary or recording imports. They prioritize short, common requests and questions over reading practice. They have not received a native-speaker editorial review.

The new lessons show spoken tone changes for 一 and 不 to support listening, while retaining dictionary marks for third-tone syllables. This is a teaching convention rather than strict citation-tone pinyin. The underlying rules were checked against the [Chinese Language Institute's tone-change guide](https://studycli.org/learn-chinese/tone-changes-in-mandarin/). No article text or audio was copied.

Speech is generated from Chinese text by the device's Mandarin voice. Voice availability, accent, clarity and offline support still need validation on the intended iPhone; content checks do not establish audio quality.

Listening Garden uses a simple heuristic: missed phrases return after 10 minutes; successful due reviews progress through 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days. Early successful repeats keep the existing due date. This is not a calibrated measure of fluency. Reading-mode answers do not affect listening review. Progress stays in the existing local backup system.

Phrase IDs combine the lesson ID and Chinese text, so reordering or editing translations preserves review history. Changing Chinese text creates a new review identity. Preserve lesson IDs when editing content.
